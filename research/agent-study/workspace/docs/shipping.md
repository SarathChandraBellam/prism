# Shipping

- UK mainland: 3.95, free over 60.00. Delivery in 2 to 4 working days.
- Ireland: 9.50 flat. Delivery in 5 to 8 working days. No free-shipping threshold.
- Rest of EU: not available yet.
- An order is "late" when it has not arrived 7 days after it shipped. Open a courier trace and offer a reshipment.
