# Customer reply

- Greet the customer by first name ("Hi Sam,").
- Say what happens next in the first two sentences.
- Never use the word "unfortunately". Do not blame the customer or the courier.
- Maximum 120 words. Plain sentences, no bullet points.
- Sign off with exactly: `— Kettle & Crumb Support`
- Check the facts first (order, ticket or policy) with the tools. Do not promise a refund that the refund rules do not allow.
