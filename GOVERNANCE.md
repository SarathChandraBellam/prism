# Governance

Prism currently follows a **maintainer-led** model.

- **Maintainers** review and merge contributions, triage issues, and accept or reject ADRs. They are listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md).
- **Decisions** are made by consensus among maintainers on the relevant issue or pull request. Significant design decisions are recorded as [ADRs](docs/adr/).
- **New maintainers** are nominated by an existing maintainer after sustained, high-quality contributions, and are added by consensus.

This model will be revisited as the contributor base grows.
